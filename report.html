Which chatbot for pitchgenius.ai

Only three routes meet the flowchart, because the flowchart's hard part is writing a qualified lead into GoHighLevel with the visitor's own words, not answering questions: GoHighLevel's own Conversation AI ($97/mo flat), Botpress with its GoHighLevel integration ($215 to $1,500/mo), or our own agent on the server's existing GoHighLevel client and AI gateway (about $13 to $250/mo in model cost at 3,000 conversations, plus build time). Test those three first in this lab; every other vendor here is a benchmark for answer quality.

Do now

  • Fix llms.txt before training any bot on it. The live file still says "No live OAuth CRM sync in this build" while the product connects HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce and GoHighLevel per seat. Any bot trained on it will tell buyers something false.
  • Every bot must say it is an AI at the start. California's B.O.T. Act (Bus. & Prof. Code §17941) and EU AI Act Article 50, in force since 2026-08-02, both require it.
  • A bot pays for itself with one Solo sale a month ($299). Anything above that per month needs volume to justify it.
  • Three decisions the flowchart leaves open: what the Individual seller is offered (the price page has Solo and Teams only), whether pilots book into the "investor-introduction" calendar the site uses today, and whether existing customers get support answers or a hand-off to support@.

What the earlier Grok analysis got wrong

  • Its core rule, "LLM only owns phrasing" with deterministic slots, contradicts the flowchart itself, which gives the agent the job of detecting intent and identifying the visitor.
  • It said Botpress has no GoHighLevel integration. Botpress's own hub lists one, maintained by Botpress (contacts, opportunities, appointments).
  • It never named the GoHighLevel-native bots (CloseBot, Stammer.ai, Asyntai, Assistable.ai), the category closest to this stack.
  • It proposed RB2B or Warmly to identify visitors; the site already loads ZoomInfo's visitor tag.
  • "DeepSeek V4 Flash" does not exist. Qualified has no "Growth" tier. Crisp's AI starts at Mini ($45), not Plus. Typebot is FSL, not AGPL. Landbot's "Professional" numbers were the Starter plan's. Voiceflow no longer publishes prices.
  • It billed GoHighLevel Conversation AI per reply; GoHighLevel bills per token. Its MCP "tool list" is really four tools that reach 550+ operations.
  • It had no legal layer and no page-speed or abuse budget.

What our category runs today

  • 1mind video avatars: Clari, Outreach, Salesloft (the Drift successor, six figures a year).

  • Warmly: Sybill. Default: Attention.

  • No chat widget at all: Lavender, Regie.ai, Avoma, Momentum, Nooks, Seamless.AI, Lusha, ZoomInfo, Fireflies.

Half the category runs no chat. The enterprise players that do pay for an avatar product; nobody in the sample runs a GoHighLevel-native bot, so a PitchGenius agent that qualifies in the buyer's own words would not look like anyone else's.

How the lab decides

  • 38 scenarios from the flowchart (4 intents, 4 persona scripts, non-fits, pricing, objections, booking, prompt injection, context, language), sent the same way to every bot.
  • 53 criteria from the flowchart, the product facts and the law, each 0-100 with a note, scored by people reading the answers. The lab computes no winner.
  • The Events tab shows what each widget exposes and how many requests it adds to the page.

Two questions that decide the routes, now answered

  • Our own agent can hand a chat to a person inside GoHighLevel. A marketplace app can register a custom conversation provider: it shows up as its own channel in the Conversations tab, visitor messages go in through the Add Inbound Message API, and a person's reply in GoHighLevel is posted to our webhook (marketplace.gohighlevel.com/docs/marketplace-modules/ConversationProviders, scope conversations/message.write).
  • Botpress writes tags and custom fields: its Upsert Contact action passes a raw JSON object straight to GoHighLevel's upsert (github.com/botpress/growth, integrations/gohighlevel/src/misc/custom-schemas.ts line 52). It has no dedicated tag action.
